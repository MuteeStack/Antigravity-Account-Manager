Our SEO Process

A systematic approach to climbing the search rankings.

1

Comprehensive Audit

We analyze your current site's technical health, backlink profile, and content gaps. We identify critical errors holding back your performance and establish benchmark metrics.

2

Technical Foundation

Before creating new content, we fix the foundation. This involves resolving crawl errors, optimizing site speed, implementing schema markup, and fixing mobile usability issues.

3

Keyword & Content Strategy

We map out high-intent keywords against the buyer's journey. We then develop a content calendar designed to build topical authority and capture featured snippets.

4

Ongoing Optimization & Reporting

SEO is never "done." We continuously monitor rankings, analyze user behavior, adjust strategies based on algorithm updates, and provide transparent monthly reporting.
